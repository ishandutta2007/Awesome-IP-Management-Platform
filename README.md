# Awesome-IP-Management-Platform

## Top IP Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Patent & Trademark Portfolio Management, Docketing, Annuities, Invention Disclosure, Analytics & IP Lifecycle*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **IP Management** (Intellectual Property Management). These systems handle invention disclosure, patent and trademark docketing, portfolio tracking, annuity payments, prosecution workflows, and IP analytics for corporations and law firms.



**Examples** include Anaqua, CPA Global FoundationIP, IPfolio, PatSnap, Alt Legal, Dennemeyer, Inteum, Questel Orbit, MaxVal IP, Gridlogics, Equinox, Patricia IP Management, PatSeer, and InnovationQ Plus (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise IP management and docketing platforms are almost entirely commercial. The strongest open option is **phpIP** for portfolio and docketing needs. Patent search and information tools (PatZilla and related) plus general document/knowledge systems can support lighter workflows. This section lists the available projects and is realistic about the significant gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[phpIP](https://github.com/jjdejong/phpip)**  

  Open-source patent and IP rights portfolio manager and docketing system (GPL) designed by patent attorneys for law-firm and portfolio needs. One of the few dedicated open IP management tools.



- **[PatZilla / IP Tools](https://github.com/ip-tools/patzilla)**  

  Modular open-source patent information research platform and data integration toolkit with access to patent data sources and a modern UI.



- **[Patent data and OPS-related open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community libraries and scripts that interact with EPO Open Patent Services and other public patent data APIs.



- **[Document and knowledge-base systems adapted for IP](https://github.com/)**  

  Open platforms such as Outline, BookStack, or Wiki.js used to organize invention disclosures, playbooks, and internal IP knowledge.



- **[Docketing and deadline tracking open scripts](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight calendar, reminder, and rule-based tools that teams extend for basic deadline management.



- **[Patent analytics open notebooks](https://github.com/)**  

  Jupyter and data-science projects for citation analysis, landscape mapping, and portfolio metrics using public patent data.



- **[Trademark and brand monitoring open experiments](https://github.com/)**  

  Community tools for watching trademark databases and alerting on potential conflicts.



- **[General case/matter management open systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Open legal practice or matter-management platforms that can be configured for simpler IP workflows.



- **[Annuity and payment tracking spreadsheets / scripts](https://github.com/)**  

  Controlled open approaches for tracking renewal deadlines when full commercial annuity services are not required.



- **[Custom CRM + document management stacks for IP](https://github.com/)**  

  Combinations of open CRM, document storage, and workflow tools used by smaller teams to approximate portfolio visibility.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Evaluating **phpIP** when you need a self-hosted portfolio and docketing system and have development capacity to adapt it.

- Using public patent databases + open search tools (PatZilla and similar) for research and landscape work.

- Building lightweight disclosure and tracking processes on open knowledge bases and workflow tools for early-stage or small portfolios.

- Accepting that complex multi-jurisdiction docketing rules, annuity services, formal prosecution integration, and enterprise analytics still require commercial IP management platforms.

- Combining open search/analytics with commercial docketing for a hybrid cost-effective approach.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture disclosures in an open knowledge base or form system → track matters and deadlines in phpIP or a structured database → enrich with public patent data and open analytics → manage documents in versioned storage. This works for smaller or specialized portfolios. Commercial platforms (Anaqua, FoundationIP, IPfolio, Questel, PatSnap, Alt Legal, etc.) remain the practical choice for law firms and corporations that need reliable global docketing, compliance, and portfolio intelligence at scale.

## Disclaimer

Always involve qualified IP counsel and verify official patent/trademark office requirements. This list is not legal advice.
